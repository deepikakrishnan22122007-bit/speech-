Microphone not working?
  1. Open this page in Google Chrome, not inside WhatsApp, Instagram or another app.
  2. Tap the icon at the left of the address bar, choose Permissions, set Microphone to Allow, then refresh the page.
  3. On your phone go to Settings, Apps, Chrome, Permissions, Microphone and choose Allow.
  4. Check that the internet is on and no other app (a call or voice recorder) is using the microphone.
  5. The address should start with https://. Do not open the file directly from your Downloads folder.

Works best in Google Chrome. Your browser will ask for microphone permission the first time.

About the App

A simple way to turn speech into text

Speech to Text lets you talk instead of type. It listens through your microphone and writes what you say.

How to Use

Four easy steps

You can be up and running in a few seconds.

  1. Open the app in Google Chrome

    Speech recognition works best in Chrome on your phone or laptop, with an internet connection.

  2. Tap "Start Speaking"

    Allow microphone access when your browser asks. The button turns red while it is listening.

  3. Speak clearly

    Say a sentence and pause. Your words appear in the text box.

  4. Copy or clear

    Tap "Copy Text" to use your text elsewhere, or "Clear" to start over.

Tips for better results

  • Speak in a quiet place and hold the phone a little closer.
  • Speak at a steady pace and pause between sentences.
  • If nothing happens, check that the microphone is allowed in your browser settings.
